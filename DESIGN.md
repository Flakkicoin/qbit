## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, futuristic interface for an intelligent physical-digital conduit: a luxury personal AI mirror. The aesthetic bridges high-performance quantum computing diagnostics with personal biological harmony and ambient digital living. It captures the psychological threshold between the user's reflection and their augmented persona, instilling sensations of hyper-clarity, effortless intelligence, and serene sophistication.

The primary target audience comprises technological visionaries, biohackers, luxury design enthusiasts, and wellness devotees who demand seamless hardware-software synthesis. The experience must feel telepathic, surgical, and cinematic—avoiding cheap cyberpunk grit in favor of pristine, refractive precision.

The style unites **Glassmorphism** and **Cybernetic Minimalist HUD** paradigms:
- **Prismatic Luminance:** Surfaces behave like crystallized, edge-lit fused silica, catching iridescent luminescence only when energized.
- **Deep Void Isolation:** OLED-black foundations guarantee that UI elements melt into ambient room lighting when inactive, leaving natural physical reflection unobstructed.
- **Data Crystallography:** Information density balances clean, airy spatial margins with microscopic, high-density quantum telemetry and monospaced system metrics.

## Colors

The chromatic structure is built on deep OLED light negation, allowing spectral quantum accents to pierce through the obsidian glass with optical clarity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Void Foundations (Neutral):** Core surface `#07090E` provides an absolute low-light baseline, ensuring ambient reflection remains clear. Elevated glass layers step gracefully through `#0D1117` and translucent alpha-layered states (`rgba(13, 17, 23, 0.65)` to `rgba(22, 27, 34, 0.4)`).
- **Quantum Cyan (Primary - `#00F2FE`):** Represents core AI communication, focus states, primary intelligence feeds, and critical HUD targeting. Pairs with `#4FACFE` for linear spectral transitions.
- **Electric Violet (Secondary - `#7F00FF`):** Represents deep quantum processing, bio-rhythm sync, and subjective/intuitive machine insights. Shifts into `#E100FF` for peak photonic excitation.
- **Neon Emerald (Tertiary - `#00F5A0`):** Denotes optimal biological homeostasis, positive telemetry, confirmation pulses, and energy stability. Supported by deep quantum emerald `#00D26A`.
- **Text & Foreground:** Spectral White `#F0F6FC` delivers primary clarity; Muted Quantum Slate `#8B949E` anchors secondary metrics; Deep Carbon `#30363D` provides low-visibility structural dividers.

### Spectral Glow Mechanics
Never flood surfaces with solid fills; colors are deployed as luminous edges, directional chromatic linear gradients, micro-glows, and prismatic refraction lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a dual presence: geometric human-centric clarity via Outfit and Inter, coupled with cold, computational telemetry delivered by JetBrains Mono.

- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Sculpted, geometric, and modern. Letterforms exhibit generous apertures and crystalline contours that project authority and futuristic optimism.
- **Core Interface & Prose (Inter):** Neutral, hyper-legible, engineered for dynamic micro-contrast against semi-transparent reflective backgrounds.
- **Telemetry, System Stats & Status Badges (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced precision anchors biometric feedback, AI states, timecodes, and quantum sensor readings, maintaining aligned numerical tracking and authentic instrumentation appeal.

## Layout & Spacing

Because the mirror display functions both as a mirror and an interactive surface, visual density is disciplined and edge-distributed. The central zone is kept naturally free of opaque UI obstruction to prioritize user reflection, while contextual UI widgets anchor to dynamic perimeter clusters.

### Spatial Model
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column adaptive fluid grid on desktop/mirror surfaces with a 40px margin perimeter. Mobile companion view adapts to a unified 4-column layout with 16px margins.
- **Safe Reflection Core:** Central viewport preserves an interactive "reflection zone" with minimal visual interference, pushing status dashboards, bio-readouts, and ambient widgets to vertical side rails and unobtrusive top/bottom docks.
- **Rhythmic Scale:** Built on a rigorous 4px baseline (`0.25rem` multiples). Component gaps utilize `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled diagnostic nodes, `space-md` (16px) for module stacking, and `space-xl` (40px) to separate independent subsystem monitors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional drop shadows in favor of fused silica glassmorphism and photonic back-glows.

- **Base Void:** Level 0 sits at absolute `#07090E` (0% opacity overlay), entirely light-absorbing.
- **Glass Panel Surface (Level 1):** `rgba(13, 17, 23, 0.65)` layered with a 20px backdrop Gaussian blur and a 1px composite border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Quantum Surface (Level 2):** Elevated interactive widgets feature a directional linear border gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.4), rgba(127, 0, 255, 0.1) 60%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03))`), backed by an ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 30px -10px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.15)`.
- **Transient State / Focus (Level 3):** Modal overlays, active AI vocalizers, and critical diagnostic alerts trigger a high-energy rim glow: `0 0 20px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.25), 0 0 40px rgba(127, 0, 255, 0.15)`, simulating light diffusing through polished optical glass.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances industrial cybernetic discipline with refined luxury consumer electronics.

- **Default Radii:** Base interactive components feature clean `0.5rem` (8px) corners, avoiding overly aggressive organic curves while remaining soft to physical touch.
- **Structural Modules & Glass Pods:** Larger cards and panels leverage `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to emulate machined curved crystal tablets.
- **Targeting Reticles & Sensor Badges:** Micro-indicators maintain slight chamfered or rounded edges (`0.25rem` / 4px), projecting calibrated instrumentation feel.
- **Biometric Rings & AI Cores:** Full circular forms (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for omnidirectional AI listening rings, iris scans, and radial wellness dials.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Quantum Button:** Surface rendered in `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.1)` with a 1px border in `#00F2FE`. Text set in JetBrains Mono `label-quantum` uppercase with spectral cyan glow. Hover/active state ignites an inner luminosity transition with `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Surface rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with a neutral edge `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. On hover, the border shifts to a dual violet-cyan gradient.
- **Haptic Glass Pill:** Compact button with pill rounding (`9999px`), subtle frosted fill, and inline status micro-LED.

### Telemetry Cards & Glass Pods
- **Diagnostic Panel:** Framed in deep charcoal translucency with backdrop blur filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`). The top border carries an asymmetric 1px prismatic gradient line from Cyan `#00F2FE` fading into electric Violet `#7F00FF` over the first 40% of the card width.
- **Module Headers:** Left-aligned JetBrains Mono label accompanied by a pulsing 4px quantum dot indicator indicating live real-time synchronization.

### Inputs & Sensor Field Sliders
- **Quantum Input Field:** Clean monolithic glass slab (`rgba(13, 17, 23, 0.8)`). Idle borders maintain an understated `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Focus shifts border to `#00F2FE` with a soft 8px outer focus glow. Text rendered in high-legibility Inter.
- **Range & Spectrum Sliders:** Ultra-slim 2px track in `#161B22` featuring a glowing iridescent scrubber thumb (`#00F2FE` with violet bloom trail).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Bio-Metric Chips:** Micro-pills framed by a 1px boundary in `#00F5A0` for nominal readings, `#00F2FE` for information sync, and `#E100FF` for deep sleep/neural states. Backgrounds are ultra-low saturation tints (`rgba(0, 245, 160, 0.08)`).
- **Quantum State Indicators:** Text rendered in JetBrains Mono (`10px`), uppercase, with letter-spacing `0.08em`.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Telemetry List:** Borderless rows separated by 1px faint horizontal dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`). Each item features a timestamp in JetBrains Mono, primary content in Inter, and an aligned metric right-aligned with monospace accuracy.

### Specialized Mirror Components
- **Spectral Iris AI Ring:** Radial pulsing orb with dual counter-rotating gradient rings (`#00F2FE` to `#7F00FF`) indicating active natural language processing or audio intake.
- **Reflection Grid Lines:** Subtle alignment crosshairs (`rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.2)`) used selectively during posture or face scan calibration modes.
